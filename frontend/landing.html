FAQ

Common questions

How accurate is the crop diagnosis?

Results are AI-assisted and based on the photo alone, so they can be wrong. They are not a laboratory diagnosis — confirm serious problems with a local agricultural expert.

Where do the mandi prices come from?

Prices come from published data.gov.in / AGMARKNET records. Data is daily, and missing dates mean no record was published for that market.

Which image formats can I upload?

JPG, PNG and WebP, up to 8 MB. A clear, well-lit photo of the affected leaf gives the best result.

Can buyers and farmers pay inside the app?

No. The marketplace is for listings, offers and chat. The AI assistant cannot accept offers or make payments on your behalf.

Is my report history private?

Yes. Only your own reports are returned to your account.
